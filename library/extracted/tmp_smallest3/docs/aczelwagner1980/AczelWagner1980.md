Suppose that there are k decision variables and n individuals and we denote by x.l the opinion of individual j as to the most appropriate value of variable i. In many of these problems (such as the allocation of a fixed sum of money among k competing projects) the column sums of the matrix (x.l) are required to have a common value s.
