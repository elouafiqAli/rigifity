## II. Exhaustive Mathematical Verification

Theorem 11 asserts that if $G \colon C \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies:
$$p G(x_1) + (1-p) G(x_2) = G\big(p x_1 + (1-p) x_2\big) \qquad (\forall x_1, x_2 \in C, \, \forall p \in [0, 1])$$
then $G(x) = a(x - x_0) + b$ for some linear functional $a \colon \operatorname{span}(C-C) \to \mathbb{R}$ and $b \in \mathbb{R}$.

We walk through and verify each of the three steps in the proof:

### Step 1: Real Homogeneity on the Shifted Set $D$
* **Proof Mechanics:** Fix $x_0 \in C$. Let $D := C - x_0$ (convex, containing $0$). Define $A(v) := G(x_0 + v) - G(x_0)$ for $v \in D$, meaning $A(0) = 0$. For any $v \in D$ and $p \in [0, 1]$, substituting $x_1 = x_0 + v$, $x_2 = x_0$, and $p$ into the functional equation yields:
  $$p G(x_0 + v) + (1-p) G(x_0) = G\big(p(x_0 + v) + (1-p)x_0\big) = G(x_0 + p v)$$
  Subtracting $G(x_0)$ from both sides:
  $$p \big(G(x_0 + v) - G(x_0)\big) = G(x_0 + p v) - G(x_0) \implies A(pv) = p A(v)$$
* **Verification:** This step is a direct application of the univariate Theorem 1 (`thm:main`) along the line segment connecting $x_0$ and $x_0 + v$. The algebraic simplification is **100% correct**.

### Step 2: Restricted Additivity
* **Proof Mechanics:** For $v, w \in D$, the functional equation at $x_1 = x_0 + v$, $x_2 = x_0 + w$, and $p = 1/2$ yields:
  $$\frac{1}{2} G(x_0 + v) + \frac{1}{2} G(x_0 + w) = G\left(x_0 + \frac{v+w}{2}\right)$$
  Subtracting $G(x_0)$:
  $$\frac{1}{2} A(v) + \frac{1}{2} A(w) = A\left(\frac{v+w}{2}\right)$$
  If the sum satisfies $v+w \in D$, then $\frac{v+w}{2} \in D$, and using Step 1 homogeneity with $p = 1/2 \in [0, 1]$ yields:
  $$A\left(\frac{v+w}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{2} A(v+w)$$
  Equating the two gives:
  $$\frac{1}{2} A(v) + \frac{1}{2} A(w) = \frac{1}{2} A(v+w) \implies A(v+w) = A(v) + A(w)$$
* **Verification:** This restricted additivity is the exact analogue of Cauchy's equation on restricted domains. The deduction holds **strictly where $v, w, v+w \in D$**.

### Step 3: Cone Extension and Full Linearization
* **Proof Mechanics:** Let the positive cone generated by $D$ be $D^* := \bigcup_{\lambda > 0} \lambda D$. Define $\hat{A} \colon D^* \to \mathbb{R}$ by $\hat{A}(\lambda v) := \lambda A(v)$ for $v \in D, \, \lambda > 0$.
  * **Well-definedness:** If $\lambda v = \mu w$ with $v, w \in D$ and $\lambda, \mu > 0$, choose $t > 0$ such that $t\lambda \le 1$ and $t\mu \le 1$. Since $D$ is convex and contains $0$, we have $t\lambda v \in D$ and $t\mu w \in D$. Then:
    $$A(t\lambda v) = t\lambda A(v) \quad \text{and} \quad A(t\mu w) = t\mu A(w)$$
    Since $t\lambda v = t\mu w$, their images under $A$ are equal:
    $$t\lambda A(v) = t\mu A(w) \implies \lambda A(v) = \mu A(w)$$
    which establishes that $\hat{A}$ is well-defined.
  * **Additivity on $D^*$:** Let $u_1, u_2 \in D^*$, so $u_1 = \lambda_1 v_1$ and $u_2 = \lambda_2 v_2$ for $v_1, v_2 \in D$ and $\lambda_1, \lambda_2 > 0$. Let $\Lambda = \lambda_1 + \lambda_2$. By convexity of $D$:
    $$v_3 := \frac{\lambda_1}{\Lambda} v_1 + \frac{\lambda_2}{\Lambda} v_2 \in D$$
    Thus, $u_1 + u_2 = \Lambda v_3 \in D^*$, and:
    $$\hat{A}(u_1 + u_2) = \Lambda A(v_3) = \Lambda \left[ A\left(\frac{\lambda_1}{\Lambda} v_1\right) + A\left(\frac{\lambda_2}{\Lambda} v_2\right) \right] = \Lambda \left[ \frac{\lambda_1}{\Lambda} A(v_1) + \frac{\lambda_2}{\Lambda} A(v_2) \right] = \lambda_1 A(v_1) + \lambda_2 A(v_2) = \hat{A}(u_1) + \hat{A}(u_2)$$
  * **Vector Space Extension ($W = D^* - D^*$):** For $u = v - w \in \operatorname{span}(C-C)$ with $v, w \in D^*$, define $a(u) := \hat{A}(v) - \hat{A}(w)$. Well-definedness holds since if $v - w = v' - w'$ then $v + w' = v' + w \implies \hat{A}(v) + \hat{A}(w') = \hat{A}(v') + \hat{A}(w)$.
  * **Full Linear Homogeneity:** Positive homogeneity holds by construction. For negative scalars, $a(-u) = a(0 - u) = -a(u)$. Thus, $a$ is a fully linear functional on $W = \operatorname{span}(C-C)$, and for any $x \in C$, $x-x_0 \in D \subseteq D^*$, so $G(x) - G(x_0) = a(x - x_0)$ holds.
* **Verification:** This cone-extension technique is mathematically brilliant. It completely bypasses standard multi-dimensional coordinate inductions. Every algebraic step is **flawless**.
